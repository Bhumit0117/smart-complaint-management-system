HOW IT WORKS

From concern to closure in three clear steps.

1. Submit

Describe the issue, choose a category and priority, and attach optional photo evidence.

2. Route

Admin teams triage the report and assign it to the appropriate department.

3. Resolve

Department staff update progress while students follow the complaint timeline.

Frontend prototype only. Real authentication, database notifications, and AI categorisation are future enhancements.
